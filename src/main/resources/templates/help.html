Help and Support

If you are unsure how to pay your fine, this page will guide you through it step by step.

What you will need

Before you start, make sure you have:

  • Your reference number - this is printed on the letter you received from TV Licensing
  • The postcode for the address the fine was issued to
  • A debit or credit card to make the payment

How to pay your fine

  1. On the home page, enter your fine reference number and postcode
  2. Click Find My Fine
  3. Check the fine details are correct
  4. Click Pay Now and enter your card details
  5. Click Confirm Payment - you will see a confirmation screen when your payment is complete

Where is my fine reference number?

Your reference number is on the letter sent to you by TV Licensing. It starts with REF-followed by six characters, for example REF-12AB34.

I would rather not pay online

You can also pay your fine by phone or by post.

By phone: Call us on 028 1234 5678 Monday to Friday, 8am to 8pm.
